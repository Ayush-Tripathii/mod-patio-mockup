About Mod Patio

Your outdoor journey
starts with Mod Patio

Mod Patio is proud to be recognized among the world's leading outdoor furniture brands, crafted with excellence in India as part of the Make in India initiative. As a complete solution provider for outdoor living, we specialize in customized designs that reflect the unique style and requirements of each client. Our commitment to sustainability is at the heart of everything we do—using non-toxic, 100% recyclable outdoor fiber while upholding uncompromised quality and innovation. By blending aesthetics, durability, and eco-responsibility, Mod Patio stands as the trusted partner for those who demand premium outdoor furniture with purpose.
